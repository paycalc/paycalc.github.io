How your pay works

▾

Award vs agreement

You’re paid whichever is higher — the agreement can’t pay under the parent award EBA 2.18(6). Since the 2025 State Wage Case the award rates sit above the EBA’s final rates, so the award is what you’re actually paid right now.

Consolidated shift allowance

26.96% for OO3–OO5, 27.46% for OO6, on ordinary earnings. It replaces night and weekend penalties because the 12-hour roster covers all hours — so it isn’t paid on overtime or public-holiday penalty hours EBA 2.9.

Flat allowances cap at 76 hours

TSV, operational, retention and laundry are paid per ordinary hour up to a full 76-hour fortnight. Operational also carries its own $379/fn ceiling.

Leave — what you keep

Annual and long service keep shift, operational, retention and TSV, so a leave fortnight lands within a few dollars of a worked one. Sick keeps retention (and TSV) only — the on-shift allowances stop EBA 2.9(6).

Salary sacrifice & the gross-up

Concessional super is taxed 15% in the fund, so a straight 5% only lands 4.25%. Grossing up sends 5% ÷ 0.85 = 5.88% so the full 5% still lands — and because it leaves before income tax you still bank more than paying it after tax.

Employer super

12.75% of ordinary-time earnings — ordinary hours, shift allowance, ordinary-time allowances and paid leave included; overtime and laundry excluded Super Reg 2023. Paid whether or not you make the member contribution.

Withheld isn’t owed

Payday tax is an estimate built for a steady income. Big-overtime, leave or backpay fortnights push it high or low — the real figure reconciles at your tax return.
