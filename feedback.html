6 Aug 2026

A cleaner, quicker page

A round of visual improvements across the site: one-tap buttons that fill the common fortnights (36–76 hours), download and save now at the top of the calculator, a slimmer layout on phones, and links to the official award, agreement and tax documents on the Rates and Pay Guide pages.

28 Jul 2026

Qualified? Pick a Q classification

The qualification allowance is now built into the pay rate, the same way payroll pays it. If you're qualified, just pick L3-Q to L6-Q from the classification list — the old Qualification setting is gone, so there's one less thing to set.

28 Jul 2026

Member super: closer to your payslip

The 5% member contribution — pre-tax or after-tax — now deducts exactly the way payroll does it, so the calculator lines up with your payslip that bit better.

23 Jul 2026

Feedback form is live

You can now send suggestions and rate corrections straight from this page. Thanks to everyone who asked for a way to give feedback.
