How your pay actually works

Real rates, what applies when, and where each rule comes from. Award = Youth Detention Centre Employees Award – State 2016 · EBA = Certified Agreement CB/2023/139.

The basics

Award vs agreement

You’re paid whichever is better — the agreement can’t pay under the parent award EBA 2.18(6). Since the 2025 State Wage Case the award rates sit about 1.5% above the EBA’s final rates, so the award is what you’re actually paid.

How a fortnight comes together

Base pay is rostered hours × your rate — a full fortnight is 76 ordinary hours. Allowances stack on top: percentage (shift allowance, on ordinary earnings) and flat (TSV, operational, retention, laundry — per ordinary hour, capped at 76).

Gross vs taxable

Gross is everything before deductions. Anything salary-sacrificed comes off before tax, so taxable income can be lower than gross — that gap is where packaging saves you money.

Casual loading

25% on top of the permanent rate Award 8.3(c), paid instead of annual, sick and long-service leave, notice and redundancy. It’s part of your ordinary rate, so shift allowance and super are calculated on the loaded rate.

Minimum engagements

Casual and part-time engagements carry a 2-hour minimum, recall to duty 2 hours, overtime on a day off 3 hours Award 8, 18. The calculator takes raw hours — enter the minimum, not the shorter time worked.

The allowances

Consolidated shift allowance (CSA)

26.96% for OO3–OO5, 27.46% for OO6, on ordinary-time earnings EBA 2.9(4). Replaces night/weekend penalties because the 12-hour roster covers all hours. Not paid on overtime or public-holiday penalty hours 2.9(6).

TSV locality

$43.40/fn with dependants, $21.70 without Dir 16/18. Townsville-based permanent staff only — casuals and Brisbane-based staff get none. Continues through paid leave.

Operational

$4.9867 per ordinary hour, capped at $379.00/fn — exactly 76 hours’ worth EBA 2.10(3). Paid on annual, long service and special leave, but not sick leave 2.10(4)–(5).

Retention

$45.00/fn after 2 years’ continuous service EBA 2.12. Doesn’t index with pay rises 2.12(5); paid on all paid leave, sick included. On paper it’s all-purpose (2.12(2)(i)); the calculator pays it flat to 76 hours.

Laundry

$6.10/fn spread across ordinary hours Award 13.6(b), CPI-adjusted each State Wage Case. Only for hours actually worked.

Qualification

Flat fortnightly amount at the top paypoint of your level for 12 months EBA 4.2: Cert IV $41.50 (OO3) · Diploma $42.80 (OO4/OO5) · Adv Diploma $44.60 (OO6).

In-charge

$15.65 per shift for the shift supervisor when most of the shift falls outside 0830–1700 — in practice, OPS1 on nights Award 13.2.

Other taxable earnings

A flexible taxable line — most commonly a secondee travel/meal allowance. Added to gross and taxed as wages, excluded from the super base. A genuine tax-free reimbursement doesn’t belong here.

Penalties & extras

Overtime

Hours beyond the rostered shift at ×2 Award 18. No CSA on top — the double rate is the compensation.

Public holidays worked

×2.5 for working the day itself Award 23.1, with a 4-hour minimum: for a short call-in, enter at least 4 hours even if you worked less.

Quad time

×4 — the ceiling rate, mainly overtime worked on a public holiday.

Higher duties

Acting at a higher classification pays that classification’s rate for the hours acted, penalties included. Kicks in after one full shift for Youth Worker→Section Supervisor and Section→Shift Supervisor; after more than 3 consecutive days for other combinations. HD pays the first paypoint of the higher level EBA 12.7.

Public holiday on your RDO

A public holiday landing on a rostered day off pays an extra day’s wage — 7.6 hrs at ordinary rates for full-timers, your usual day for part-timers Award 23.4. Permanents only.

Leave — what you keep, what stops

Annual & long service

Base rate for rostered hours — continuous shift workers accrue five weeks a year (the 5th week is the QES shiftworker entitlement). Keeps shift allowance through both — paid in lieu of leave loading EBA 2.9(5), and at 26.96–27.46% it’s worth more. Also keeps operational 2.10(5), retention 2.12 and TSV. No laundry or in-charge — those only exist for shifts actually worked.

Leave loading — who gets it

Continuous shift workers don’t get the separate annual leave loading — the CSA continuing through leave is the loading, and it pays better. Day workers are on classic loading: 17.5% on four weeks, or 14% across five EBA 2.13.

Sick / carer’s

Base rate. Keeps retention and TSV. Doesn’t get shift allowance EBA 2.9(6)(iv), operational 2.10(4)(iii) or laundry — a sick fortnight pays noticeably less than a worked one.

Special (paid)

Base rate. Keeps operational, retention, TSV. Doesn’t get shift allowance or laundry.

Casuals & leave

No paid leave — the 25% loading is the trade Award 8.3(e).

Tax & super

PAYG withholding

ATO Schedule 1 coefficients (tables dated 01.07.2026), applied per fortnight. Scale 2 = tax-free threshold claimed, normal for your main job. It’s a per-pay estimate — the true figure settles at your return.

Study loans (HELP/STSL)

ATO Schedule 8 — nothing withheld until taxable pay reaches about $2,674/fn (threshold claimed). Heads-up: salary sacrificing lowers the fortnightly STSL withheld but not the annual repayment (repayment income adds sacrificed super back).

Salary sacrifice & the gross-up

Concessional super is taxed 15% in the fund, so a straight 5% only lands 4.25%. Grossing up sends 5% ÷ 0.85 = 5.88% so the full 5% still lands — and because it leaves before income tax you still bank more than paying it after tax.

Concessional cap

Employer contributions plus everything salary-sacrificed count toward $32,500/yr (FY2026-27). After-tax member contributions don’t.

Employer super

12.75% of ordinary-time earnings — ordinary hours, shift allowance, ordinary-time allowances and paid leave included; overtime and laundry excluded Super Reg 2023. Paid whether or not you make the member contribution. The CSA sits in the base only to the extent the SG requires it (OTE) EBA 2.9(7).

Member 5%

The standard employee contribution for permanents — after-tax by default, or packaged pre-tax through Remserv. Casuals default to 0%.

Good to know

Backpay & manual payments

Lump sums are withheld using the ATO’s lump-sum method — roughly, taxed as if you earned that much every fortnight — so the payment usually looks overtaxed on the day. The overs come back at your tax return.

Withheld isn’t owed

Payday tax is an estimate built for a steady income. Uneven fortnights (big OT, leave, backpay) push it high or low — the real number reconciles at your return.

Check a payslip now and then

The payslip-check section on the calculator takes gross, tax, net and employer super from a real payslip and flags any drift beyond 50 cents.
