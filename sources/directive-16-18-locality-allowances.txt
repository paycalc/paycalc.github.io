 
DIRECTIVE 16/18 
 
Supersedes: 19/99 
 
Minister for Industrial Relations Directive: 
Locality Allowances 
 
 
1. Purpose: 
To prescribe the locality allowances payable to officers and employees specified in this directive 
who are appointed to work at named centres. 
 
2. Effective date:   11 May 2018 
3. Legislative authority:   
Section 54(1) of the Public Service Act 2008. 
Sections 51 and 52 of the Public Service Act 2008 and section 941 of the Industrial Relations Act 
2016 apply if there is a conflict with an act, regulation or industrial instrument.  
4. Application:  
This directive applies to: 
 public service officers; and 
 temporary employees engaged under section 148(2)(a) of the Public Service Act 2008. 
 
This directive does not apply to: 
 employees engaged on a casual basis under sections 147(2)(b) or 148(2)(b) of the Public Service 
Act 2008; or 
 general employees engaged under section 147(2)(a) of the Public Service Act 2008. 
 
5. Previous references:  
 Directives 19/99; 12/97:  
 Sections 63 and 67 of the Public Service Management and Employment Regulation 1988  
 Determination No. 11 
 Circular letters 10/91, 6/90 
 Administrative Instruction No. 1 I 6

GENERAL CONDITIONS 
 
 
6. Entitlement 
 
6.1  A public service officer or temporary employee engaged under section 148(2)(a) of the Public 
Service Act 2008 ( appointed to a centre listed in this 
schedule shall be paid - 
 
 the full rate of locality allowance for the centre if the employee satisfies the chief executive 
that he or she has a dependent spouse, dependent de facto spouse or dependent child; or 
 
 one-half of the full rate of locality allowance for the centre if the employee does not have a 
dependent spouse, dependent de facto spouse or dependent child. 
 
7. Dependants  
 
7.1  Departments are responsible for establishing their own criteria to determine whether an employee 
is eligible for the full rate of locality allowance. As a guide, a person in receipt of remuneration less 
than the Queensland Minimum Wage ($727.50 per week for award employees as at 1 September 
2017) could be regarded as a dependant. 
 
8. Spouse eligible for locality allowance 
 
8.1  One-half of the full rate of locality allowance shall be paid to an employee, even though the 
employee has a dependent child, whose spouse or de facto spouse lives with the employee and -  
 
 is also an employee; or 
 
 is employed by a corporation sole, statutory corporation, board, authority or other body 
constituted under any Act; and is eligible to receive a locality allowance as a result of that 
employment. 
 
9. Notification of change of circumstances 
 
9.1 An employee receiving a locality allowance shall notify the chief executive immediately of any 

payable. 
 
10. Payable during leave with salary 
 
10.1  Locality allowance shall be paid to an employee absent on recreation leave, sick leave, long 
service leave or on any other leave on full salary. 
 
11. Not payable during leave without salary 
 
11.1  Locality allowance shall not be paid to an employee absent on leave without salary. 
 
12. Divisional or district allowance 
 
12.1 An employee stationed at a construction project shall be paid so much of the locality allowance 
ordinarily payable to that employee as exceeds the total amount paid to that employee for - 
 
 any divisional or district allowance prescribed for the centre generally in any industrial award
