Q means qualified. Payroll doesn’t pay the qualification allowance EBA 4.2 as its own line — it adds it to the top paypoint of your level and stores the result as one classification. So L3-Q is L3-4 plus the OO3 allowance: $2,721.00 + $41.50 = $2,762.50 a fortnight. The top paypoint is the only one the allowance is payable at, so these four cover everyone entitled to it — which is why the calculator now offers them as classifications instead of a separate Qualification setting. Only L3-Q has been seen on a payslip (13–26 Jun 2026, which pays it casual at $45.43586). The other three are the same arithmetic on their level’s top paypoint, using the allowance this site maps to that level — right if that mapping is right, but unconfirmed. One 4Q, 5Q or 6Q payslip would settle it. Payroll calls these YDZO3Q, YDZO4Q and YDZO5Q; the calculator lists them as L3-Q to L6-Q so they sort with the rest of the scale. Whether a 6Q code exists is unknown — the OO6 entitlement isn’t in doubt, just the name. Work them off the fortnightly, not off the permanent hourly: $2,762.50 ÷ 76 × 1.25 gives the casual $45.43586, where rounding the permanent rate first and then multiplying comes out a hundred-thousandth low. Because the allowance sits inside the rate it is pro-rated by the hours you work, picks up the casual 25%, and counts toward the shift allowance — worth about $11.19 a fortnight more than paying it flat, for a full-time OO3.

Sources

where each number comes from

Pay scale 01.09.2025

Youth Detention Centre Employees Award – State 2016, reprinted after the 2025 State Wage Case (3.5%) — matter B/2025/49–50, certified by the Registrar on 5 September 2025. All 15 fortnightly figures were checked against the reprint’s own clause 12.2 table, which prints them as whole dollars, and the hourly rate is fortnightly ÷ 76 because that is how the award defines a casual hour Award 8.3(c). Two of the fifteen were then confirmed again against a real payslip.

Allowances EBA CB/2023/139

Shift, operational, retention and qualification from the certified agreement; in-charge and laundry from the award; TSV from Directive 16/18.

Tax tables ATO 01.07.2026

Schedule 1 (PAYG) and Schedule 8 (STSL) for FY2026-27, including the 16%→15% bottom-rate cut. Both checked line by line against the ATO’s own schedules — all five withholding scales the calculator offers, and both study-loan tables, match exactly: every threshold and both coefficients. Study loans use the marginal repayment system that started in 2025-26, with nothing withheld below $1,337 a week.

Super 01.07.2026

Employer 12.75% of OTE — ordinary time earnings including shift loadings, ordinary-time allowances (laundry included), paid leave and public holidays worked (Superannuation (State Public Sector) Regulation 2023); overtime excluded. Confirmed against a payslip whose employer contribution was exactly 12.75% of the full gross. Concessional cap $32,500 from 01.07.2026.

Directives Public Sector Act 2022

Where the award and agreement stop, a Queensland Government directive takes over. The ones that reach this calculator: Locality Allowances 16/18 (the TSV rate above) · Special Leave 12/24 and Higher Duties 16/24 — both from 30.09.2024, replacing 05/17 and 04/20 · Recreation Leave 11/24 (annual leave and the 17.5% loading) · Domestic Travelling and Relieving Expenses 13/23 (travel and meal payments, which belong on the calculator’s “other taxable earnings” line). All are published on forgov.qld.gov.au.

Checked against the instruments 26.07.2026

Every rate and rule here was read against the primary document it comes from — the award reprint, the certified agreement, ATO Schedule 1, and the five directives — and all of it matched. The verification register records what was checked, which version, and the dates that would make it stale. Every figure the calculator uses has now been checked at source. The one question that had stayed open — whether recreation leave should carry 27.5% instead of the shift allowance — was settled against a pay summary in July 2026: the shift allowance is what’s paid. The documents themselves aren’t hosted here; use the official copies at qirc.qld.gov.au, forgov.qld.gov.au and ato.gov.au.

watch this space
1 Aug 2026

YDC 2026 agreement — offered, not certified

The current agreement (CB/2023/139, certified 7 Dec 2023) reaches its nominal expiry on 31 July 2026 — it keeps operating until replaced. The offer is 3.5% (incl. 0.5% CPI uplift, already triggered) then 2.5% + 2.5%, conditional on all unions agreeing in-principle by 31 Aug 2026. Not applied until certified — the rise builds a separate agreement ladder against the award floor (cl 2.18(6)), it is never added to the award table.

~1 Sep 2026

State Wage Case 2026

Moves the award pay scale, and the in-charge allowance with it Award 13.7(a). The expense allowances — laundry, overtime meal, motor vehicle — move separately, by CPI (Eight Capitals, ABS 6401.0 Table 7) 13.7(b)–(c), so don’t apply the wage-case percentage to those. Round each new fortnightly salary to a whole dollar before dividing by 76.

Mar CPI

CPI uplift watch (years 2 & 3)

If March “All Groups Brisbane” CPI beats the guaranteed rise, a top-up applies (max +0.5% yr 1 — already triggered; +1% yrs 2–3, so 2.5% could become up to 3.5%).

1 Jul 2027

Next tax cut

Bottom marginal rate drops 15%→14%. The ATO reissues Schedule 1 and the tax tables get replaced.
