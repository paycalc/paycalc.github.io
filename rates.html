where each number comes from

Pay scale 01.09.2025

Youth Detention Centre Employees Award – State 2016, reprinted after the 2025 State Wage Case (3.5%). Verified row-by-row against the QIRC reprint fortnightly figures.

Allowances EBA CB/2023/139

Shift, operational, retention and qualification from the certified agreement; in-charge and laundry from the award; TSV from Directive 16/18.

Tax tables ATO 01.07.2026

Schedule 1 (PAYG) and Schedule 8 (STSL) for FY2026-27, including the 16%→15% bottom-rate cut. Validated against the ATO’s worked examples.

Super 01.07.2026

Employer 12.75% of OTE — ordinary time earnings including shift loadings, ordinary-time allowances and paid leave (Superannuation (State Public Sector) Regulation 2023). Concessional cap $32,500 from 01.07.2026.

Incoming — not applied yet

watch this space
1 Aug 2026

YDC 2026 agreement — offered, not certified

3.5% (incl. 0.5% CPI uplift, already triggered) then 2.5% + 2.5%, conditional on all unions agreeing in-principle by 31 Aug 2026. Not applied until certified — the rise builds a separate agreement ladder against the award floor (cl 2.18(6)), it is never added to the award table.

~1 Sep 2026

State Wage Case 2026

Moves the award pay scale and the expense allowances (in-charge, laundry).

Mar CPI

CPI uplift watch (years 2 & 3)

If March “All Groups Brisbane” CPI beats the guaranteed rise, a top-up applies (max +0.5% yr 1 — already triggered; +1% yrs 2–3, so 2.5% could become up to 3.5%).

1 Jul 2027

Next tax cut

Bottom marginal rate drops 15%→14%. The ATO reissues Schedule 1 and the tax tables get replaced.

On certification

Also in the offer

COLA payments (cl 2.2) dropped — nothing carried over. New explicit handover-overtime clause — no rate change, overtime already computes at ×2; it confirms eligibility to claim it.
